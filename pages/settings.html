Library sync

Choose where this device keeps its synced library, or keep everything on this device only. Devices only sync with devices that use the same place.

The full address of the file, including its name. The folder is created if it does not exist.

Stored only on this device, never synced. Prefer an app password where your server offers one. Your password manager can fill this in and save it.

Encryption Optional

Encrypt the synced library so that only your own devices can read it. The library stored on each device is not changed.

At least 10 characters; a few unrelated words work well. You need it once on every device. You also get 5 recovery keys in case you forget it. A password manager can generate and store the passphrase for you.

Forgot the passphrase? Use a recovery key

Each recovery key works once. Other devices that are already unlocked keep working.

Lost the passphrase and every recovery key?

You can replace the synced copy with this device's library, encrypted with a new passphrase. Whatever only exists in the unreadable copy is lost. Your other devices keep their libraries: once they unlock with the new passphrase, their entries are merged back in.

Library & automation

Show what to read today: titles with a new chapter come first, then the ones you are still catching up on. A title leaves the list when you have read up to it, and returns with the next release. You can pin a title to a position. Needs an RSS feed linked to the title. Off by default; your manual queue is kept.

Ask AniList and MangaUpdates for the other names of a saved title, so a feed that uses a different name is not treated as a duplicate. Only the title is sent. Off by default.

Link the free MangaUpdates release feed to every title the lookup matched, so you hear about new chapters even when the site you read on has no feed. A title matched on AniList is first searched on MangaUpdates by its title. Titles are linked 8 a minute, so a big library takes a few minutes. A title without a cover image gets the one from MangaUpdates, also a title linked before; a title with an image keeps it. New chapters link to MangaUpdates, not to the site you read on, and its chapter numbers can differ from that site's. Unsubscribing a title stops this for it. Needs "Look up alt titles online".
